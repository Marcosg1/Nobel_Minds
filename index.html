Nobel Minds

By Marcos Gallegos

Nobel laureates are presented here across the years covered by this dataset, from the earliest prizes through the 2014 award year. The compilation is a historical snapshot and does not include later prizes or revisions published by the Nobel Prize organization. The material is organized so that award history may be examined by discipline, geography, and period, and so that individual recipients can be understood in relation to broader patterns in the prize record.

For authoritative information on how the prizes are awarded, eligibility, and related policies, refer to the frequently asked questions published by the Nobel Prize.
